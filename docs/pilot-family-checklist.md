# Family pilot: try Hustle Builder together

Prepared November 22, 2026. For 2–3 invited families, including at least one family with more than one kid. This is a checklist for a pilot; it is not a record of completed participant testing.

## Invitation Steve can send

> Would your family help me try [Hustle Builder](https://hustlebuilder.org/)? Your kid can build a small business page, and you control the parent account and publishing. Then we will use clearly marked practice bookings to try the emails, flyer QR code, calendars, changes, and cancellations. No real job or customer payment is involved. Allow about 30–45 minutes together, plus a later calendar check. Please tell me where you get confused or stuck. You do not need to buy a plan or order printed flyers. I can arrange eligible pilot access privately before you publish.

## Before you start

- [ ] Choose a parent, a kid, and an adult practice customer. The customer can use another browser/device and their own inbox. Keep sign-in codes and private booking/calendar links private.
- [ ] Steve confirms pilot access privately. Do not buy a season or year plan for this test. If using the normal free trial instead, the parent must choose it knowingly: there is a real **$0.50 card charge**, and Hustle Builder requests a full refund through Stripe right away to the same card. A bank may take time to show the credit, or the charge may disappear. This helps confirm that an adult is approving and managing the page; it does not prove a family relationship.
- [ ] Use first names and a town. Use a clearly labeled practice business and booking notes: **“TEST ONLY — no real visit, work, or customer payment.”** Use your own contact details, not another person's. Do not add home addresses or identifying photos to the public page.
- [ ] Record the parent and customer devices, browser, calendar app, and time zone. Use future appointment times that nobody will mistake for real work.

## 1. Kid builds; parent publishes

1. Open [hustlebuilder.org](https://hustlebuilder.org/) and build a draft. Choose work, business name, services, prices, colors, and an available time. Check that the preview matches the choices, including pet-care pages and both looks.
2. At **Go live**, try **Email my parent an invitation**. Check that the email explains the next step. The invitation alone does not move a draft to another device: use the original device to review it, or the app's explicit transfer/backup option.
3. The parent continues, verifies their own email, reads the notices, and decides whether to publish. Use the pilot access arranged with Steve, or the normal trial only if the parent agreed to its refunded card charge. Stop if publishing would require an unwanted purchase.
4. Open the public website in a separate browser. Check the name, prices, photos, availability, and request form. The parent's private email and phone should not appear on the public page.
5. For a family with multiple kids: open **Parent account & saved pages → Your account → Start a new page**. Publish a different business for another kid under the same parent. Switch back and confirm each kid keeps their own name, services, prices, design, and website link.

## 2. Save and scan a flyer

1. Open **Flyers**. Try two designs, including an Ink saver option. Change a design detail, save a printable PDF, and open the saved file. Check the name, prices, and QR code.
2. Scan the saved PDF's QR code with another phone. Check that it opens **this kid's public website**, not the builder, another sibling, or the Taylor demo. Also try the website link in the publication email and its flyer/editor link.
3. If possible, print one copy at 100% / Actual size, US Letter, portrait. Scan the paper copy. Record whether you tested paper or only the screen.
4. Try **Print at a shop**. Save the PDF and open a shop link if useful. The PDF must be uploaded manually on the shop's site. No order or payment is needed for the pilot.

## 3. Try a customer's request, a time change, and cancellation

Use an email address you control so both sides can check receipts. These are practice appointments only.

1. Customer: request one offered service and a future time. Include the test-only note. Check the acknowledgment, quoted details, and **Manage booking** link.
2. Parent: check the notification and **My jobs**. Confirm the request. Customer: check the confirmation. Compare the service, day, time, and time zone on both sides.
3. Subscribe to the family calendar using the app/email calendar options. A subscription receives later updates; importing a single downloaded file may create a separate copy. Record when the request/confirmation appears in Google Calendar or Apple Calendar.
4. Customer: use **Manage booking** to request another available time. Parent: decline it first. Check that the original appointment remains. Request another time and have the parent approve it. Check both emails, My jobs, and the calendar after it refreshes.
5. Customer: cancel through **Manage booking**. Check that the app marks it cancelled and both parties receive email. After calendar refresh, record whether the event disappears or shows as cancelled. Deleting or declining a calendar copy alone does not cancel the Hustle Builder booking.

## 4. Approve and cancel repeating visits

Try this with at least one family, using a separate practice request so the single-booking checks above stay easy to follow.

1. Customer: request **Every week** or **Every other week**. Parent: check that it is still just one appointment before approval. Choose a total of 2–8 visits, including the original booking; four is the default. Review the first appointment and total, then choose **Approve repeating visits**. Check that the right number of appointments and email notices are created.
2. If trying pet care while away, choose 2–14 away dates, including both the first and last day, and put the first appointment on the first away date. Approval creates one daily visit throughout that range, including the original visit. There is no separate visit-count selector for this option.
3. Check that repeating visits keep the same local clock time. If your dates cross a daylight-saving change, the local appointment time should stay the same; the UTC offset can change.
4. Cancel one visit through its individual management action. Check that the other visits remain. Then use **Cancel remaining visits**. The confirmation should explain that it cancels future, uncompleted visits in that series; completed/past visits and unrelated bookings stay unchanged. Check both parties' emails and the calendar after refresh.

## 5. Kid asks; parent decides

1. Make a second clearly marked practice request and have the parent confirm it. In a multi-kid family, make this request for the other kid's business.
2. Parent: in **My jobs**, use **Copy family jobs code**. Save it privately, then choose **Your account → Sign out on this device** before handing over the device. Kid: open **My jobs → Use a family jobs code**, enter the eight-character **Family jobs code**, and choose **View family requests**. This code allows family request viewing and asking the parent; it does not unlock the parent's saved builder or grant parent approval controls. It can show all siblings, so do not put it on a flyer or public page.
3. Kid: choose **Ask my parent to cancel** and confirm the request. The booking should stay active while it waits for the parent.
4. Parent: open the review email, choose **Keep booking**, and check that the cancellation request is dismissed without cancelling the job.
5. Kid: ask again. Parent: cancel this time. Check the app, customer email, and calendar update. No automatic text message is promised; if a customer supplied only a phone number, the parent must contact them directly.
6. Multi-kid parent: use **Show bookings for → All kids**, then each kid's filter. Check that bookings belong to the right business, including a kid with no bookings. On an individual parent booking page, try **View all family bookings** and sign in if asked. Cancelling one sibling's job must not change another's.

## 6. Finish on a shared device

1. Parent: use **Your account → Sign out on this device**. Refresh and reopen My jobs/account. Check that parent controls, the account's private details, and private calendar links are no longer visible. If a family jobs code is still open, choose **Forget family jobs code** too; its limited request access is separate from a parent session.
2. Sign back in as the same parent and check that the saved family pages return. Do not give a kid the parent's email code or leave the parent signed in when testing the kid's approval-request flow.
3. Cancel any remaining practice bookings and remove unwanted practice calendar subscriptions/copies. Ask Steve to help remove public practice pages if you do not want them left live.

## Tell Steve what happened

- Where did you hesitate, need help, or expect a different next step?
- Did the kid's draft, sibling switcher, and parent approval make sense?
- Did every email and QR code open the right business or booking?
- Which calendar app did you use, and how long did each update take? Did a duplicate or wrong-time event remain?
- Could you save/open/print the PDF? Did scanning paper work? Did approving/cancelling repeating visits match your expectations?
- After sign-out, could you still see any private family information or approve anything?

For a problem, record the step, device/browser, what you expected, and what appeared. Share a cropped screenshot with private contact details, email codes, booking-management links, and calendar-feed links hidden.

## Completion criteria

Each family can build and publish without an unintended purchase; scan a working business QR code; receive correct parent/customer emails; confirm, reschedule, and cancel; and sign out cleanly. At least one multi-kid family verifies page switching, booking filters, sibling isolation, and repeating visits. Record Google and Apple results separately, including refresh delays. An untested item stays **Not tested**. No real work, customer payment, paid print order, or independent pilot result is implied by completing this document.
