# Parent verification wording

The trial's parent-verification payment is a **$0.50 card charge followed by a full refund to the same card**. Describe the charge and refund explicitly in the builder, parent FAQ, invitation emails, policies, Stripe checkout and receipt descriptions, and payment-status messages.

The app requests the refund through Stripe right away. The cardholder's bank may take time to show the credit; when a charge is quickly reversed, the original charge may disappear instead. Do not promise that the credit will be visible immediately.

The visible “Why do we ask?” disclosure explains that the process helps confirm that an adult approves and manages the child's page. A parent or guardian agrees to run the account and handle booking requests. A card payment does not establish legal guardianship, so avoid claiming that it proves who the parent is.

A completed plan payment or eligible playtester code skips the separate $0.50 payment. This copy update changes no payment amounts, refund calls, consent records, or playtester behavior.
