Questions

Is it safe?

The public page shows a first name and a town. A parent’s email and phone stay off the page. Every booking request goes to the parent.

What does it cost?

$9.99 for the whole winter, or $19.99 for a year. The first 14 days after publish are free.

Does it auto-renew?

No. A plan is a one-time payment. You are never charged automatically.

What is the 50¢?

A parent who starts the free trial checks a card for 50¢. Stripe emails a receipt, and we refund it right away. Buying a plan skips that check.

Who gets paid for a job?

Neighbors pay the family directly. Hustle Builder does not collect, hold, or guarantee job payments.

Can we stop or get a refund?

A parent can take the page down at any time. A plan can be refunded within 14 days of the payment.

Connect your family printer

Hustle Builder can’t talk to a printer by itself. Your phone or computer does that part, and it takes about a minute.

  1. iPhone or iPad: tap Save PDF to print, then tap Share and Print. Pick your printer. Most Wi-Fi printers show up on their own when you’re on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Android: save the PDF, open it, tap the menu, then Print. If your printer isn’t listed, a parent can add it once in Settings under Printing.
  3. Computer: save the PDF, open it, press Print, and pick the printer.
  4. Settings that matter: Letter paper, scale 100% or Actual size, and Black and white for Ink saver. Print one test copy first.
  5. No printer? Send the PDF to a parent’s computer, or ask your library or a print shop to print it from your phone.
