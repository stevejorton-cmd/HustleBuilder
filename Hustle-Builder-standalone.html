Connect your family printer

Hustle Builder can’t talk to a printer by itself. Your phone or computer does that part, and it takes about a minute.

  1. iPhone or iPad: tap Save PDF to print, then tap Share and Print. Pick your printer. Most Wi-Fi printers show up on their own when you’re on the same Wi-Fi.
  2. Android: save the PDF, open it, tap the menu, then Print. If your printer isn’t listed, a parent can add it once in Settings under Printing.
  3. Computer: save the PDF, open it, press Print, and pick the printer.
  4. Settings that matter: Letter paper, scale 100% or Actual size, and Black and white for Ink saver. Print one test copy first.
  5. No printer? Send the PDF to a parent’s computer, or ask your library or a print shop to print it from your phone.
